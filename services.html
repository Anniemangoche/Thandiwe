Plunge pool

A raised pool between the cottages and the shoreline, with the hills behind it — open to all guests through the day.

Lakeside bar

Drinks served from a repurposed dugout canoe a few steps from the water, under an old mango tree.

Lake lounge

A shaded, thatched-roof sitting area with wicker seating and a television, looking straight out over the water.

Private verandas

Each cottage opens onto its own brick veranda with wicker armchairs facing the garden.

Kitchenette

A small kitchen available on request, for guests who prefer to prepare their own meals.

Garden gazebo

A quiet, shaded corner of the garden with wicker chairs — a good spot to read through the afternoon.
