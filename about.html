About the lodge

A family-run stretch of the Mangochi shoreline.

Built low, built close to the water, built to last.

Thandiwe Lodge sits directly on the shore of Lake Malawi in Mangochi — a cluster of thatched-roof cottages, a shared garden running down to the beach, and a bar built out of a hollowed canoe under an old mango tree. It's a small place, kept simple on purpose: comfortable rooms, honest food, and a view of the lake from almost everywhere you stand.

Our setting

The lake is the reason people come back.

The grounds

A garden that runs straight to the beach.

Lawns, hibiscus hedges and mango trees separate the cottages from the shoreline, with a swing set and picnic tables for the quieter hours of the day.

The cottages

Thatched roofs, brick walls, kept close together.

Each cottage has its own entrance and veranda, arranged along a shared path so the lodge still feels like one place rather than scattered units.

Our approach

Simple, well kept, and close to the water — that's the whole idea.

We're not trying to be a resort. Thandiwe Lodge is a small, well-kept lakeside stop for people who want a proper view and a comfortable bed, without the extras driving up the price.
